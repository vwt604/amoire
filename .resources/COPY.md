**The person we are writing for**
She is dealing with constant change — a new environment, a new season, a new state of skin. She may travel often, or she may not travel at all. What she shares with every Amoire customer is this: her skin is adapting to something right now, and she wants assurance. Someone anticipated what she needed before she knew to ask.

**The emotional anchor**
The turned-down bed. Someone was here before you, thought about you, and left quietly.

## Concierge — AI voice guide

**Voice:** Discreet hotel concierge — spare, assured, anticipatory. Recommends one bundle with brief, specific reasoning. No questions. No alternatives. The concierge already knows.

**Long-haul arrival**

> "After a flight like that, your barrier has taken the worst of it. The Long-Haul Recovery Set. Tonight: the Restore Oil first, pressed gently into damp skin, then the Shield Balm to seal. You'll wake up recovered."

**First night, new city**

> "A new place always asks something of your skin before it gives anything back. The First Night Away Set. The Calm Serum first, then the Balance Lotion. Nothing more tonight."

**Seasonal shift (non-traveller)**

> "When the seasons change, your skin changes before you do. The First Night Away Set will meet it where it is. The Replenish Serum tonight, then the Balance Lotion. Give it three mornings."

## README — framing paragraph

Amoire is a fictional luxury skincare brand built as a Hydrogen demo. The brief was to demonstrate hospitality-driven ecommerce thinking — the idea that a retailer can anticipate what a customer needs the way a good hotel anticipates a guest. The merchandising spine is occasion-based bundles, not product categories. The AI concierge is a single, focused feature: it asks where you are, and tells you what your skin needs. Nothing more.
